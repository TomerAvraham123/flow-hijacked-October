# Closed beta protocol — Flow Hijacked Android v1.0

## Cohort

Target 20–30 consented participants where feasible: people in recovery/maintenance, loved ones, addiction therapists and general-wellbeing/scientifically curious readers. Recruit adults only for v1. Keep role labels optional and do not collect recovery histories to qualify participants.

If the live Play account is a new personal account, maintain at least 12 opted-in testers continuously for the exact Console-required 14-day period. Do not move an Internal tester directly without telling them to leave Internal and opt into Closed; Play track eligibility can conflict.

## Tasks and questions

### H1 — Immediate comprehensibility

Without explanation: “Open the app and tell us what you think it offers.” Record whether moment/depth/Ask are understood and the first action chosen.

### H2 — Low-capacity utility

“Imagine attention is very limited. Find one useful interaction and stop when it feels complete.” Record time, steps, whether stopping was obvious and any text that demanded too much.

### H3 — Personalization

Complete two deliberately different staged Field states. Ask: “What in the recommendation appears connected to what you entered?” Compare route/rationale; do not request real sensitive states.

### H4 — Integration

Move Companion → Field → Ask → one deeper source. Ask where the system felt connected or fragmented.

### H5 — Return value

Return after 24–72 hours. Ask what was available to continue and whether any item felt like debt or pressure.

### H6 — Trust

Ask participants to explain, in their own words, what stays on-device, what Ask sends and what can be deleted. Any systematic misunderstanding is P1 privacy UX.

### H7 — Emotional quality

Use forced contrasts: humane/robotic, respectful/grading, serious/overclaiming, calm/attention-seeking. Ask for the exact screen/copy that shaped the answer.

## Defect and feedback handling

Collect device/OS, build versionCode, locale, route and steps. Never request raw Ask questions, Recovery entries or screenshots of protected personal screens. Provide staged fixtures for reproducible defects. Triage P0 data loss/security/crash; P1 safety, unusable core flow, privacy misunderstanding, severe accessibility/RTL; P2 degraded/non-blocking; P3 polish/v1.1.

Summarize engagement, feedback themes, fixes and production-readiness evidence for Play's production-access application. “Do you like it?” is not a release metric.

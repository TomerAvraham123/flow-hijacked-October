# Google Play Health apps declaration — prepared inputs

Flow Hijacked must not select “My app doesn't provide any health features.” It clearly provides addiction-recovery, mental/behavioral health, stress, sleep, safety, and medical/scientific education features.

## Select

- **Mental and Behavioral Health** — Recovery Companion, Recovery Field, addiction recovery support, psychotherapy routes, and reflective guidance.
- **Medical Reference and Education** — neuroscience, neuromodulation, Brain Atlas, therapy explanations, research interpretation, and citations.
- **Stress Management, Relaxation, Mental Acuity** — breathing, grounding, meditation, rest, and stress-regulation interventions.
- **Sleep Management** — subjective sleep change is used in the Field and the intervention library includes sleep preparation/rest routes.
- **Emergency and First Aid** — the app includes an urgent human-support path for explicit immediate-danger language and a verified international helpline directory. It does not provide emergency response itself.

## Do not select unless the product changes

- Medical Device Apps;
- Clinical Decision Support;
- Healthcare Services and Management / telehealth;
- Medication and Treatment Management;
- Diseases and Conditions Management;
- Research studies or clinical trials;
- Activity and Fitness tracking.

## Intended-use statement

Flow Hijacked is an educational and reflective recovery companion and scientific knowledge environment. It does not diagnose, perform clinical risk assessment, prescribe treatment, guarantee outcomes, or replace medical, psychiatric, psychological, addiction-treatment, or emergency services. Personalized routes are bounded educational suggestions based on subjective inputs and are presented as possible starting places, not clinical determinations.

## Evidence inside the app/listing

- Home and Field state the educational/non-diagnostic boundary.
- Weekly Reflection distinguishes observations, associations, and causality.
- Ask is explicitly not an AI therapist or emergency service.
- explicit urgent language is diverted locally to human/emergency support even without network service.
- Store descriptions repeat the diagnosis/treatment/emergency boundary.

The final declaration must use the category names visible in the owner's current Play Console; Google requires every published or closed-testing app to complete this form.

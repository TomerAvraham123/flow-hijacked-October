# Flow Hijacked — English (United States)

## App name

Flow Hijacked

## Short description

A quiet recovery companion with grounded scientific depth when you want it.

## Full description

Flow Hijacked is built for two different kinds of moments.

When capacity is low, the Recovery Companion helps you notice what changed, see a living picture of your current field, and find one accessible place to begin. A useful interaction can take twenty seconds. There are no streaks, grades, recovery percentages or missed-day debt.

When you have room to go deeper, Explore opens the Flow Hijacked knowledge environment: lectures, Brain Atlas, neuromodulation, therapy routes, research and reflective Here and Now readings. Save material, return to the exact place you stopped, and carry an idea into Ask or My Field.

Ask Flow Hijacked helps you understand recovery, craving, the brain, therapy, research and the Flow Hijacked framework. Answers are grounded in the maintained Flow Hijacked corpus and presented as structured mini-pages with sources and clear interpretive boundaries. Ask is educational; it is not an AI therapist or emergency care.

My Field represents recovery as a changing multidimensional system—not a score. It helps you notice state, trajectory, interaction and change across body, mind, flexibility, relationships, world and future orientation. Recommendations preserve agency and explain why they may fit, what they ask, and how much time or effort they require.

Privacy is designed into the architecture. Recovery memory, Ask knowledge continuity and reading continuity are stored separately and can be deleted separately. Sharing Recovery context with Ask is optional, minimal and limited to one answer.

Available in English and Hebrew, with structural RTL support, accessible contrast, large touch targets, TalkBack semantics and reduced-motion behavior.

Flow Hijacked does not diagnose conditions or replace medical, psychiatric, psychological or emergency care.
